### NSF SBIR/STTR Phase I

- Current full-proposal dates include November 4, 2026 and March 4, 2027
- Maximum Phase I award: up to $305,000
- Mandatory first step: submit an NSF Project Pitch and receive an invitation before a full Phase I proposal
- Missing requirements:
  - eligible U.S. small business with fewer than 500 employees
  - at least 51% qualifying U.S. ownership and control
  - PI employed by the company more than 50% during the award
  - defensible deep-technology innovation beyond a BirdNET dashboard
  - customer discovery and a credible commercial market
  - technical R&D objectives, risks, milestones, and budget
- Milestone to unlock: incorporate, complete ten customer interviews, define and test a novel technical claim, and submit a Project Pitch; March 2027 is more credible than November 2026

## Milestone sequence

1. Obtain one written reply from a scientific or site partner.
2. Secure permission for three to five pilot sites.
3. Name a qualified ecological reviewer and finalize the annotation/error protocol.
4. Collect 50 to 100 real recordings with complete effort and context metadata.
5. Publish detection precision/error results and unresolved cases.
6. Obtain one site-partner letter and one scientific letter of support.
7. Choose a legal route: U.S. company, nonprofit fiscal sponsor, or partner-led application.
8. Complete ten structured customer/beneficiary interviews.
9. Document BirdNET/model licensing and data/publication permissions.
10. Prepare the January 2027 Cornell application or an invited NSF Project Pitch, depending on the chosen legal and partner path.

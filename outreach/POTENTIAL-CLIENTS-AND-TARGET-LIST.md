inclusion means only that the organization may have a relevant use case. It does
not establish need, budget, authority, interest, or product fit.

Do not infer a person's name, title, email address, active project, protected
species, or regulatory obligation. Record each item from an authoritative public
source before using it in outreach.

## Qualification record

For every account, capture:

1. Official source URL and date checked.
2. Current project or program relevant to the inquiry.
3. Publicly confirmed contact route or person.
4. The operational question Listening.bio could help test.
5. Why a small written evaluation is appropriate now.
6. Any conflict, procurement rule, or restriction.

Pricing and savings remain hypotheses until a real pilot measures delivery cost,
review effort, turnaround, and client value.

# Vertical Pilot Conversation Kits

These kits frame research conversations. They do not contain substantiated case
studies, performance guarantees, regulatory claims, or approved pricing.

Use the complete email drafts in
`outreach/enterprise/SAFE-OUTREACH-SEQUENCES.md`. Keep communication in writing
unless Rodrigo explicitly approves a call.

## Renewable energy

**Question to test:** Can a traceable acoustic workflow make authorized field
audio easier to process, review, and compare while existing ecological and
permitting professionals retain interpretation and sign-off?

**Offer:** A small demonstration on a limited set of client-authorized WAV files.
Return the source checksum, documented model configuration, unreviewed candidate
table, review log, and limitations statement.

**Do not claim:** regulatory acceptance, continuous coverage, reduced delays,
survey replacement, protected-species absence, cost savings, or reviewer sign-off
that has not occurred.

## Environmental consultancies

**Question to test:** Can Listening.bio reduce administrative friction around PAM
ingestion, provenance, candidate triage, and exports without changing the
consultancy's scientific authority?

**Offer:** A written workflow review and a sandbox demonstration using public or
consultancy-authorized data.

**Measure in a pilot:** ingest success, processing time, reviewer time, complete
provenance rate, disagreement rate, and usefulness of exports.

**Do not claim:** five-times throughput, 80% time reduction, gross margin, client
readiness, or TNFD/ESRS compliance.

## Nature and carbon technology

**Question to test:** Can acoustic candidates provide a complementary field layer
alongside remote sensing and existing ecological methods?

**Offer:** A methods exchange followed by a public-data demonstration. Treat
soundscape indices and species candidates as research outputs whose meaning
depends on protocol, site context, and qualified review.

**Do not claim:** biodiversity-credit verification, immutable proof of uplift,
third-party assurance, or verified species richness.

## Forestry and conservation

**Question to test:** Can a repeatable, permission-based acoustic protocol support
stewardship, education, or grant reporting at a manageable cost?

**Offer:** Co-design a 30-day pilot plan for three to five sites. No field work is
represented as complete until permission, protocol, equipment, and reviewer are
confirmed.

## Commercial discovery

Ask in writing:

- What decision or report would the evidence support?
- How is audio collected, reviewed, and retained today?
- Which errors are most costly?
- Who must approve methods and findings?
- What security, privacy, procurement, and insurance requirements apply?
- What would make a limited evaluation useful?

Only propose pricing after scope, responsibilities, costs, and acceptance
criteria are understood. Record any number as a dated hypothesis, not a market
fact.

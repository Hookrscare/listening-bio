# Enterprise Claim Register

## Allowed now

- Listening.bio has a functional MVP for WAV ingestion, configured BirdNET
  inference, provenance, candidate review, maps, and data exports.
- A public Creative Commons recording has completed the configured BirdNET demo
  workflow; the source and result are documented in the repository.
- The product can generate framework-informed evidence drafts.
- SHA-256 records a file fingerprint that can later reveal whether the exact file
  changed.
- A partner pilot can measure processing time, review effort, precision on an
  agreed reference sample, and operational usefulness.

## Requires qualification every time

- BirdNET output is a model candidate, not a confirmed observation.
- Soundscape indices depend on recording protocol, implementation, habitat,
  equipment, and interpretation.
- TNFD and ESRS E4 are context for evidence organization. Listening.bio does not
  certify alignment or compliance.
- A checksum supports integrity checking; it is not by itself a chain-of-custody
  system or proof of authenticity.

## Prohibited until independently supported

- "TNFD compliant," "ESRS E4 verified," "regulatory-ready," or "USFWS approved"
- "Verified species" when review has not been completed by a qualified person
- Guaranteed savings, margins, accuracy, approval, or turnaround
- A 24-hour audit generated from 30 minutes of audio
- Claims that passive acoustics replaces required field surveys or professional
  judgment
- Client, project, deployment, or case-study claims without written permission
- Named contacts or direct addresses inferred from email patterns

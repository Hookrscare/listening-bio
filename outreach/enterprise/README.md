# Enterprise Outreach Safety Gate

This folder contains the approved working assets for enterprise outreach. Nothing
in this folder is evidence that an email was sent, a customer was engaged, or a
field result was produced.

## Current decision

The material imported from the temporary Gemini workspace is **not approved for
use**. It combined synthetic audio with hard-coded species detections and then
described the output as verified, compliant, and suitable for regulatory use. It
also included unsourced personal names, guessed email addresses, delivery-speed
claims, savings figures, and margin promises.

Those source files remain outside this repository for traceability. Do not copy,
send, publish, or present them.

## Approved positioning

Listening.bio is an early acoustic evidence workflow. It can ingest WAV files,
run configured BirdNET inference, preserve source and model provenance, support
human review, and export evidence drafts. Automated detections are candidates,
not confirmed species observations.

Framework exports may help organize evidence for a client's TNFD or ESRS E4
work. They are not disclosures, certifications, assurance opinions, regulatory
submissions, or compliance determinations.

## Required gates before outreach

1. Use only a person and direct address confirmed by an authoritative source.
2. Otherwise use the organization's official contact route and ask to be routed.
3. Personalize only from a cited public source.
4. Offer a small demonstration on client-authorized audio, not a regulatory audit.
5. Never promise accuracy, approval, savings, turnaround, or margin without
   measured evidence and written scope.
6. Keep the result marked `demonstration_only` until a qualified reviewer has
   completed the agreed review process.
7. Require Rodrigo's final approval immediately before sending each campaign.

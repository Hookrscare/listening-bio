Why It Matters

Biodiversity monitoring is too expensive and too episodic.

Many communities need ecological evidence but cannot run frequent expert surveys. BioSignal is designed as a field-to-evidence layer: recordings come in, candidate detections are traceable, reviewers can confirm or reject results, and partners can export data for analysis, reports, and grant evidence.

1

Collect

Record WAV audio at parks, campuses, wetlands, gardens, and restoration sites using a repeatable protocol.

2

Analyze

Run configured BirdNET inference and preserve raw model outputs, thresholds, timestamps, and source context.

3

Review

Confirm or reject candidate detections so the platform supports human validation instead of unchecked AI claims.

4

Export

Share CSV evidence, site summaries, maps, and pilot reports with researchers, city teams, NGOs, and funders.
